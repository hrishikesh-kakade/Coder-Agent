MS Dhoni

Biography

Mahendra Singh Dhoni, commonly known as MS Dhoni, is a former Indian cricketer and captain renowned for his calm demeanor and finishing abilities. He led India to victories in the 2007 ICC World Twenty20, 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.
